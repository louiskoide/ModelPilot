  - What each option (a)–(d) would have saved, at follow rates 0–1, on Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 at 5m and 1h. Option (a) is net of early switches at short pauses.
- **Unknown stays unknown:** transitions without usage or times are counted, never priced, as are the two around a `/compact` turn.
